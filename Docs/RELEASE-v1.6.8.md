# RestedXP-Navigator v1.6.8

## Blizzard-native world markers

World-marker heads, pins, backgrounds and strokes now come directly from Blizzard PingUI atlases. RestedXP-Navigator keeps only its oval floor ring to visually plant close-range markers on the ground.

### Mapping
- Green Assist: quest accept/turn-in, vendor, innkeeper/hearth, trainer
- Blue On My Way: movement/travel
- Orange Attack: kill/combat
- Blue NonThreat: all other objectives
